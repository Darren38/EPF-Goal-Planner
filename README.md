# EPF Goal Planner

A single page calculator that estimates when your Malaysian EPF savings will reach a target amount.

**Live:** https://darren38.github.io/EPF-Calculator/

## Features

**Goals**
- Quick presets of RM250k, RM500k, RM1m and RM2m
- EPF retirement benchmarks: Basic RM390k, Adequate RM650k, Enhanced RM1.3m
- Or set any custom target

**Contributions**
- From salary, using your share and the employer share of EPF wages
- Or a fixed monthly amount
- Employer rate follows member type, Malaysian and PR or non-Malaysian
- Annual salary growth
- Yearly extra as a voluntary top up or a bonus, with a chosen payment month

**Dividend rate**
- Fixed rate, with quick picks at 2.50, 5.50, 6.00 and 6.30 percent
- Historical average over the last 5, 10 or 20 years, or all years on record
- Trend line projection
- Replay the actual declared rates year by year
- Konvensional and Shariah rate sets

**Output**
- Headline answer in years, or as a target date or age
- Inflation adjustment to show the result in today's money
- Contribution stop age
- Split across Akaun Persaraan, Akaun Sejahtera and Akaun Fleksibel
- Year by year table, downloadable as CSV
- EPF style year end crediting or monthly compounding
- Three themes: light, dark and KWSP

## Usage

Open the live link. Everything runs in your browser and your inputs are saved locally on your own device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

To use it offline, download `index.html` and open it in any browser.

## Notes

Results are estimates based on the inputs you enter. EPF dividends are declared each year and are not guaranteed, so past rates do not promise future ones. This is not financial advice.
